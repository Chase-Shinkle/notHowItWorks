# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Defines the three seed types and the distinct walkable structure each one grows into.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Beanstalk grows tall
The system SHALL grow a beanstalk seed into a tall vertical structure that the player can climb.

#### Scenario: Climb a beanstalk
- **WHEN** a beanstalk is fully grown and the player moves into it
- **THEN** the player can climb it to its top

### Requirement: Pumpkin grows wide and low
The system SHALL grow a pumpkin seed into a wide, low block the player can stand on and step over.

#### Scenario: Step onto a pumpkin
- **WHEN** a pumpkin is fully grown and the player walks onto it
- **THEN** the player stands on top of it

### Requirement: Vine grows long and horizontal
The system SHALL grow a vine seed into a long horizontal span the player can walk across.

#### Scenario: Cross a vine
- **WHEN** a vine is fully grown across a gap and the player walks onto it
- **THEN** the player can cross the gap along it
